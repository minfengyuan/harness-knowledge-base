---
name: grilling
description: Stress-test a plan or design with focused questions when the user explicitly asks for a grilling review.
---

Stress-test the plan by resolving the decisions that materially affect scope, architecture, risk, or acceptance. For each question, provide a recommended answer.

Ask one high-leverage question at a time when the answer unlocks the next decision; group independent questions when that is clearer.

If a *fact* can be found by exploring the codebase, look it up rather than asking me. The *decisions*, though, are mine — put each one to me and wait for my answer.

Do not enact the plan until I confirm shared understanding when this skill was explicitly invoked as a pre-implementation review.
