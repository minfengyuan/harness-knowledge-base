# Storm Research

Use a small set of independent perspectives when a question benefits from adversarial comparison, broad evidence, or hidden connections. Choose the number and roles from the question and available capacity; a fixed five-agent ritual is not required.

## Prepare

- State the question, scope, time boundary, source standard, and intended decision.
- Infer safe defaults. Ask only when ambiguity would materially change the research.
- Choose perspectives that cover the relevant operational, scholarly, critical, economic, historical, or domain-specific angles. Omit irrelevant angles.
- Prefer primary and authoritative sources, verify time-sensitive claims, and label fact, source-reported view, and inference.

## Investigate

- Run independent searches in parallel when that is available and useful; use sequential research when the question is narrow or concurrency is unavailable.
- Give each researcher a focused scope and require evidence, counterevidence, limitations, and unresolved questions.
- Compare agreements, contradictions, source authority, directness, recency, and independent corroboration before synthesizing.
- Never invent citations. If browsing or a source is unavailable, state the gap plainly.

## Report

Answer the question first, then provide ranked findings, evidence strength, material disagreements, actionable recommendations, and research gaps. Label inferences and assign qualitative confidence to important conclusions. Do not force consensus where evidence remains mixed.
